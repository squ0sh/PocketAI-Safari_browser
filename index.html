Ask about text


Give the AI an article or note to discuss. It cannot automatically read your open Safari tab.




1. Add your text




Up to 100,000 characters. Long text may be shortened to fit the model; paste the section you want to discuss.



Files up to 2 MB. PDFs and images are not supported; copy their text instead.


Fetch a website online (optional)
This contacts the website directly and needs internet. Some sites block access; paste their text if fetching fails.




2. Start a conversation
This creates a new chat with the text attached. Existing conversations are kept.


3. Send a question
Choose a suggestion or write your own question in the chat. Load a model if needed, then tap Send. Nothing is sent automatically.

On this device
Run one warm-up and three measured replies with the same short prompt on each loaded local model. First-text and whole-reply token rates are medians; load times come from separate actual loads. Results stay here. Temperature, battery state and other apps affect speed. No phone model or memory capacity is guessed.


Use 27B online?
Online mode sends your question, relevant chat history and attached page text to the configured AI provider. It requires internet. You can keep using a smaller local model offline.
Checking availability contacts this app’s server without sending chat content. Switching modes does not send your question; tap Send afterwards.
