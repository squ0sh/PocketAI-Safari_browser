Take your AI offline
  1. Open the app online and wait for “App ready for offline GGUF”. Add it to your Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu.
  2. Choose “Save to Files” in the model menu. Make sure the GGUF is downloaded onto your iPhone, rather than only stored in iCloud.
  3. Turn on airplane mode, reopen Pocket AI and choose “Load from a file…”. Pick the GGUF each time you reopen the app.
Supports the listed Bonsai Q1_0 GGUF models. Other GGUF formats are not supported. 27B has offline support files but remains a desktop-class experiment.
Safari may remove website storage when space is tight. Keep your GGUF in Files. Local mode never falls back to Online Assist.
